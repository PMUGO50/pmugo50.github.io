<p style="color:#333333; font-size:15px; font-style:normal; font-family: 'Source Serif 4', 'Source Han Serif SC', 'Source Han Serif CN', 'Noto Serif CJK SC', 'Noto Serif SC', '思源宋体', serif; line-height: 1.65;">
Now, in my opinion, we must first make the following division: what is that which always is and has no becoming, and what is that which is always becoming but never is?<br>
(余以为，首当辨者：恒在而不变者何？常变而无实存者何？)<br>
<br>
The former is graspable by intellect with the aid of reason, being always the same, whereas the latter is opinable by opinion with the aid of unreasoning sensation, becoming and perishing, but never really being.<br>
(夫恒在者，智以理得之，常而不易也。夫变在者，感以见知之，其生灭无常，流转不居，而未尝有定在也。)
</p>
